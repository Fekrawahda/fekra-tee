How It Started • The Spark

مجرد فِـكـرة It Didn’t Start In A Boardroom. It Started Between Sets At The Gym.

Before the first drop, before the labels and the numbered releases, FEKRA (فكرة) was nothing more than a sudden conversation at the gym with my partner, Abdelnasser.

I had an archive of raw, personal designs I had created over time—sketches, moods, lyrics, and memories sitting untouched. Standing there talking with Abdelnasser, the realization hit us: every great piece of art begins as a fleeting, unscripted idea in someone's head.

Instead of releasing random graphics, we curated those archive designs into four distinct chapters—each named after the exact human spark that brought it to life. Today, we put those raw moments on tees and hoodies you can actually wear.

Return & Exchange Policy

14-Day Exchange & Return Window

Unworn, unwashed garments in their original packaging with tags intact may be exchanged for a different size or returned within 14 days of delivery.

7oby Custom Date & Custom Pieces

Because 7oby pairs with a custom printed anniversary date and Your Fekra Studio pieces are printed specifically to order, personalized items are final sale unless they arrive with a manufacturing or print defect. (7oby pairs ordered without a custom date remain eligible for standard 14-day size exchanges.)

How To Initiate An Exchange
